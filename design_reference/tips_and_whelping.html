restaurant_menu Nutrition Guide Week 6 Milestone

Switch to High-Density Growth Formula

From Day 42 onward, puppies grow at an exponential rate, reducing maternal stomach capacity. Transition Bella gradually over 5 days to a veterinary puppy formula containing high protein, DHA, and caloric density to sustain lactation stores.

Portion Strategy

Feed 3 to 4 smaller, frequent meals throughout the day rather than two heavy bowls.

psychology Behavioral Insight Nesting Instinct

Decoding the Nesting Frenzy

Furious blanket digging, panting, and hiding behind sofas are completely natural hormonal impulses triggered by dropping progesterone. Introduce the whelping box now with an old t-shirt bearing your personal scent to establish a calming sanctuary.

Pro Tip: Thermal Peace

Keep draft guards intact and maintain ambient whelping room temperature at 75°F–80°F.

verified Myth Busted Crucial Caution

Calcium Supplementation: When & Why?

Myth: Giving calcium pills before birth makes the puppies bones stronger.
Clinical Fact: Supplementing calcium prior to active labor actually suppresses parathyroid gland regulation, dangerously triggering life-threatening postpartum eclampsia. Only offer vet-approved calcium paste during or after active labor begins.
