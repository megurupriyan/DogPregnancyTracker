Weeks 1 – 5 Review

Fertilization, Implantation & Organogenesis
expand_more
Week 1–2 (Days 1–14): Conception to Blastocyst

Ova fertilization in oviducts, gentle cellular migration toward uterine horns. Mom displays minimal physical changes.

Week 3 (Days 15–21): Uterine Implantation

Embryos implant into uterine walls. Protective placenta formation begins; dam may experience mild morning sluggishness.

Week 4–5 (Days 22–35): Organogenesis & Heartbeat

First ultrasound verification window. Fetal eyes, spinal chords, and limb buds take shape. Embryos reached walnut size.

Weeks 7 – 9 Countdown

Coat Development, Colostrum & Labor Watch
expand_more
Week 7 (Days 43–49): Hair Growth & Calcification

Puppies develop distinct coat pigments and hair coats. Radiographs can safely verify puppy counts.

Week 8 (Days 50–56): Crowding & Colostrum Milk

Fetal movements are tangible against dam's flank! Milk/colostrum may appear; temperature charting initiates twice daily.

Week 9 (Days 57–63): Full Maturity & Whelping

Puppy lungs reach maturity. Watch for the vital rectally measured temperature drop below 99°F indicating labor within 12–24h.
